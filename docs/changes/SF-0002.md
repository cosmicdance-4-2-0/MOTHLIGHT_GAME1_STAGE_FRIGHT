## Motivation

Before gameplay coding, publish explicit *planned* boundaries and objectives that the six fellow Kestrel designers can critique independently. The owner requested ample room for experimentation, no release timetable, and modular Adventures whose Plays, Casts and visual styles may differ. A versioned documentation baseline lets us change minds without losing decision history.

## Added and changed

- `docs/ROADMAP.md`: stages 0–8 with hypotheses, exit evidence, and explicit future work not scheduled.
- `docs/TERMINOLOGY.md`: distinguish physical Room, Stage, Set and fixed camera Shot from scripted Play, Act, Scene, Beat, Cue, Production and Performance.
- `docs/contracts/V0_PLANNED_CONTRACTS.md`: C00–C12 tentative owners and boundaries; deterministic commands, Ghost/Director, NPC, Play, dialogue, Adventure composition, presentation and replay.
- `docs/design/V0_GAMEPLAY.md`: linked player loops, ghost-resource policy, NPC decisions, rehearsal versus world truth, dialogue variants, multiple outcomes and binding.
- `docs/design/V0_DECISION_REGISTER.md`: user-established directions versus integrator proposals, alternatives, falsifiable trials and unresolved questions.
- `docs/design/STAGE1_VISUAL_DIRECTION.md`: fixed-camera 2.5D pixel art proposals, alternative painted plates, initial Play Scenes versus navigable Rooms, per-Adventure Visual Profile/Art Pack independence.
- `docs/reviews/STAGE0_SIX_AGENT_HANDOFF.md` and `docs/reviews/STAGE0_REVIEW_INTAKE.md`: immutable common review source, six distinct lenses, provenance, dissent, and reporting states.
- `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `version.json`, `CHANGELOG.md` and this change record.
