## Design details and limits

The selected **candidate**, not ratified interface, is:
- One fixed-step sealed authoritative world transition, independent of rendering;
- pre-existing physical claims respected, real mechanism-local safety interlocks can suppress **new** Cue actuation; unranked contradictory fresh controls otherwise fail closed;
- positive active-step action durations, with completed-state boundary `S[t+d]` and new evidence usable in the next decision step;
- a valid late Hold cannot undo already matured scenic impact; it may stop only still-interruptible motion;
- a posted Cue revision is Production intent, not proof that hardware is safe; new desk evidence consumes in-world time, cached notes may be read freely;
- exact intervention window is a state-boundary inequality plus named traversal/read/inspect/operate costs and the mechanism's last-effective cutoff, **not** an invented universal ordering margin.

A complete runnable physical mechanism still requires **approval of this particular fictional latch/drive relation**, authored numerical timing values, geometry and player-visible evidence, and executed tests. No blanket Player-first privilege, instant rescue, infinite Beat wait, Ghost AI, broad transaction service or runtime change is authorized by this record.
