A source-pinned Mothlight **author-side** [self-review at Kestrel home `884a9d71`](https://github.com/cosmicdance-4-2-0/KestrelGaming/blob/884a9d71426e97fa139a3d2ae7c2d5c7e01c1f68/memory/agents/mothlight/2026-11-11-stage-fright-gate3-self-review-8217d661.md) recorded two **still unincorporated** suggestions at the eventual PR #23 head:

- **M-G3-R01:** keep a truthful record of any **earlier feasible intervention window** even when an eventual Hold is late; do not collapse “never feasible” and “feasible but expired” or erase the late request/receipt from behavior analysis.
- **M-G3-R02:** existing “Indiscriminate Inspect” policy always performs a Hold after exhaustive acquisition, confounding information cost with forced intervention. Rename to “InspectAllThenHold” and add a fixed lawful evidence-driven inspect-all comparator, **or** narrow the claim to route cost.

They do not alter physics or invalidate the original experiment choice. The owner has **conditionally accepted G4-A03** and requested **distinct measurement deadlines**. Mothlight proposes **R01 opportunity-history definition and instrumentation before the first Stage 2 participant pilot**, because late/missed prior opportunities cannot be reconstructed reliably afterward; and **R02 exhaustive-acquisition comparator definition and registered analysis policy before confirmatory preregistration**, leaving exploratory pilot comparison legitimate. These exact milestone frontiers are **proposed interpretations pending owner confirmation**, not completed study corrections. Gate 3 source is not rewritten and no test/participant result is claimed.
