## Motivation

Stage 0 established a candidate one-world authoritative transition (Gate 1) and a matched ordinary-fault-versus-legitimate-Cue chandelier fixture (Gate 2). Neither merged proposal constitutes a proven or ratified gameplay design. The experiment plan selected for Stage 2 identifies an eventual human judgment test but lacks a complete preregisterable procedure: independent first exposures, counterfactual scripts, coded evidence reasoning, held-out transfer, pilot separation, consent, invalid-run discipline, and an exact owner approval boundary.

**SF-0007 proposes documentation of Stage 0 Gate 3**, not participants, a result, gameplay implementation or a pre-selected population inference.

## Risks and follow-up

- The initial A/B Cue posting may perfectly reveal the two authored cases; H3-02 therefore supports **at most evidence-consistent, case-sensitive observable behavior**, not proof of cognitive understanding. Both complementary crossed conditions—**authorized/unsafe** and **unauthorized/safe**—must be preflight-valid before a **bounded within-mechanism** H3-04 claim, and neither establishes broad skill generalization.
- A threshold chosen after viewing confirmatory participant outcomes would invalidate the confirmatory claim; pilot-tuned parameters and analysis rules require an immutable preregistration lock.
- A physically correct Hold that misses the Beat is not automatically irrational. **No-Hold without a prospective decision marker is observationally unclassified**, not demonstrated intentional restraint; optional declared Refrain remains a non-authoritative observational marker, not a new world control. Analyze safety, performance, evidence acquisition, explanation and engagement separately.
- **After-run accounts are retrospective and may be outcome-contaminated; they cannot prove predecision cognition or retroactively convert unmarked inaction into declared restraint.** Mechanical replay parity does not establish sensory accessibility or why a person made a decision.
- A low-cost familiar-participant pilot can inform usability but cannot support broad statistical generalization.
- Personal data and recordings require specific consent and retention permissions **before** Stage 2; never publish identifiable raw notes in Git.

**Gate after this integration:** Stage 0 Gate 3 may be proposed for owner acceptance after focused review of preflight, procedure, thresholds and stop rules. Stage 0 Gate 4 closeout and Stage 2 implementation remain separately authorized. Pilot/confirmatory recruitment, numerical timing/participant targets, final visual presentation and operational data-handling are later locked before actual participant exposure.
