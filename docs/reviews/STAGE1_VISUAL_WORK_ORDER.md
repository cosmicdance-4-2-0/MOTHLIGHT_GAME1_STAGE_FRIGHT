## Goal

Compare visual treatments for **one fixed four-station chandelier judgment problem**. Determine whether a player can recognize the stagehand, the mechanism, legitimate Cue evidence, ordinary fault evidence, the Hold control and resulting Show/World outcomes. A compelling picture that conceals needed evidence fails this checkpoint.

## Bounded work

1. Prepare two coherent style candidates from the [Stage 1 plan](../design/STAGE1_VISUAL_DIRECTION.md): **A full pixel theater** and **B painterly plates with a pixel cast**. Keep option C (HD pixel) as a future comparison, not a new obligation.
2. Show the **same** representative Room/Shot, four stations (VIEW/DESK/RIG/HOLD), camera and gameplay-relevant geometry in both visual treatments. Supply normal, authorized-Cue and ordinary-fault depictions as needed to inspect clue presentation. Do not bake mechanical truth into an inaccessible color-only cue.
3. Include one backstage/rigging composition to expose reach, occlusion and interaction landmarks. Compare character/prop proportions, pixel scale, depth and foreground priority at documented **actual target display sizes**; select targets before evaluation rather than inventing performance results.
4. Define provisional palette, silhouette, asset identification and presentation-only Visual Profile / Art Pack responsibilities. Preserve per-Adventure art substitution as a design goal without implementing a generalized loader.
5. Record a comparison with **observed** legibility, alternative input/sensory path, atmosphere and authoring/revision cost. Note what was measured, by whom, at what size and by what method. Do not infer human comprehension from a static render or claim that matching simulation traces prove accessible presentation.

## Reviewable checkpoint and exit

Deliver one short candidate sheet, two matched annotated Shots, a backstage/rigging view, a small profile/asset-name proposal and an evidence-and-decision table. Link original art source, generator/model where applicable, rights and transformations. Independent reviewer challenge is welcome; approval belongs to the owner.

**Positive checks:** Hazard/interactable landmarks are distinguishable; both conditions admit legible evidence without omniscient early knowledge; alternative cues remain available when effects/motion are reduced; normal/Cue/fault states can be compared without hidden mechanic changes.

**Negative checks:** A style fails if occlusion hides the necessary witness cue; a color-only warning disappears for a supported mode; costume/lighting destroys actor-vs-prop silhouette; or a presentation asset changes World authority. Unexecuted checks must be labeled proposed.

## Boundaries

- **Authorized:** visual reference research, disposable comparative prototypes and design documentation within a separately scoped checkpoint.
- **Not authorized by this work order:** Stage 2 game logic, new simulation APIs, a final/production sprite pipeline, recruiting participants, secret telemetry, asset packaging for distribution, new dependencies without review, Ghost/Director/Bell mechanics, public release or modifications to other agents' identities.
- **Kestrel art skills:** owner previously proposed developing reusable sprite-production skills first. They may support this work but are a **separate KestrelGaming assignment and review**; no unrequested cross-repository changes.
- **Hold-over conditions:** Gate 4 A02 timing/accessibility and A03 protocol R01/R02 remain open until their affected preflight gates. Stage 1 alone cannot close those conditions.
- **Revision:** failing a visual treatment calls for a documented alternative or amended Shot; no automatic engine expansion.
