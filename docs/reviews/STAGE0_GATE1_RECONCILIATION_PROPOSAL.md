## Decision rationale: the difficult choices

| Decision | Selected *candidate* | Why this choice | Preserved alternative, tradeoff / affected author |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **D1 — resource conflicts** | A pre-existing **physical ownership/reservation** in state wins; otherwise apply a *published mechanism-local physical safety interlock* if applicable; otherwise unreserved incompatible fresh writers **all lose, one unchanged resource result**. Equivalent fresh writes coalesce **only** under an explicit idempotent combination contract. | Prevents callback ordering, blanket Player privilege, accidental double actuation and a late cue overriding real, previously established control. A failed action is visible, not silently retried. | **Axiom:** valid manual override can supersede a scheduled automatic Cue; acceptable only if declared as this control's physical policy rather than global source rank. **Latch:** use trusted admission/reservation ticket order for fresh writers, even when neither holds a pre-existing physical claim; more permissive but ticket acquisition may silently encode game power/host timing. **Flint:** distinct identical-result writers should not automatically coalesce; retained as the default. **Kite/Harbor/Gauge:** physical interlock and no-effect conflict preferred. |
| **D2 — Bell vs due Cue** | A **valid Bell activation that becomes effective during this step** suppresses **new, not-yet-committed Show Cues** due in that step. It does not stop an already committed mechanism effect or cancel a previously due impact. The Show transitions to canceled after world consequences. | Gives the Bell prospective, understandable value while preserving injury history and bounded response windows. A Bell initiated too late to complete this step has no such privilege. | **Harbor/Flint/Kite:** recommend same-step Cue suppression. **Axiom:** stage Bell cancellation after world effects; its emphasis on mature harm is retained. A rule allowing due Cues to start regardless of same-tick Bell would also be deterministic, but risks an unexplained extra cue after emergency acknowledgment. |
| **D3 — Ghost Power boundary (later Stage 4)** | At future commitment, evaluate legality and affordability against **opening-tick Power**, after Director precommit eligibility and mechanism arbitration; debit **once** at actual commitment. Regen accrues at end of the tick and is spendable only at the **next** tick boundary. | Produces a distinct financial frontier, prevents a hidden same-tick regen credit financing a plan, and is straightforward to audit with exact integers/remainders. **Not a cost/rate choice or an enabled Ghost capability now.** | **Axiom** proposed regen-first, affordability-second as a provisional alternative; it would permit a plan funded by this tick's regen. | **D4 — Director privacy (later Stage 4)** | A Ghost proposes **once** from permitted Ghost observations. A narrowly scoped Director may opaque-veto/defer the candidate before commitment; it cannot retarget, attach private hints or permit same-tick replanning. | **D5 — completed-tick information** | Timed Inspect acquires **post-world** facts at its completion boundary; those facts influence actions **no earlier than the next active step**. **Priority is a physical claim, not a universal source ranking.** The program may represent these decisions with a small resource-group reducer; they do not require a generalized scheduler, transaction service, global event bus or package forest. A source-valid request is not automatically a successful physical action.

## Canonical boundary notation and admission

Let `S[t]` be the **immutable, fully committed state after t active steps**, with `t` the next step index. A step computes `Step(S[t], SealedInbox[t], ContentHash, RulesHash, RngDomains) -> (S[t+1], OrderedEvents[t], Receipts[t])`. The host may prepare input outside Step but cannot mutate authoritative world state. **One step processes [t,t+1) and publishes only S[t+1].** All time-bearing actions use **positive integer active simulation ticks** with fixture-declared units; no wall-time duration, animation frame count, arbitrary priority score or hidden default substitutes.

**Trusted command envelope** is a *semantic minimum*, not a ratified wire schema: `runId`, host-bound `issuer`, distinct `actingEntity`, `commandId`, immutable payload fingerprint, origin/provenance (UI, Show, Actor policy, later Ghost), target, verb/arguments, requested/effective step, and any authorized control claim or source-cue ID. A Play author may request its *own bound Cue*, never impersonate an Actor by typing `issuer=Mara`. A host-bound issuer is not multiplayer cryptographic authentication.

**Admission:**

1. Seal a finite inbox at boundary `S[t]`. Late input waits for a **new explicit host admission** at a later boundary, not a mid-step insert. Pause/options/focus loss stops *starting* steps; if a step has already started, finish/commit it before freezing. No catch-up after resumption.
2. Look up `(runId, trusted issuer, commandId)` in the already-admitted **idempotency ledger first**, regardless of current pause/staleness. Identical normalized payload ⇒ return original pending/final disposition, **no new effect**. Same ID with different payload ⇒ `ID_REUSE_MISMATCH`.
3. New submissions during pause ⇒ `NOT_ADMITTED_PAUSED` without reserving ID; resubmission after resume may be admitted at the same unexecuted tick. For new active submissions, stale effective tick ⇒ `REJECT_STALE`; unsupported future external tick ⇒ `REJECT_FUTURE`. An internally authored future Show Cue is a signed/configured schedule, **not an external request**.
4. Check actor/issuer authority, known target and allowed verb, range, ability, fixtures and declared duration. External invalid/rejected attempts produce truthful receipts; rejected-before-admission IDs do not become applied world commands. Once admitted, a timed job's identity is durable for that Run; on completion its *then-current* physical preconditions are checked again. Prevent duplicate or hidden revalidation retries.
5. Start an action admitted **at boundary t** and with duration `d` active steps. It consumes those `d` successful steps and, if uninterrupted, its requested physical effect is **eligible for arbitration during step `t+d-1`**, yielding a completed outcome in `S[t+d]`. Thus even `d=1` consumes one complete step; an input arriving after the seal cannot join it. An Inspect samples world truth at `S[t+d]` after the world resolver, with `availableFrom=t+d` (usable only when preparing step `t+d`). **Alternative:** Axiom's stricter rule that a newly started operation cannot affect a due Cue in its first consumed step is deliberately not assumed; it can be achieved by an authored minimum duration/earlier cutoff. Whether this toy's `HOLD` can validly complete on a due Cue step depends on the physical interlock and reachability contract in Gate 2.

**Already committed material effects** carry a due step and *last-effective-interruption boundary* in authoritative state. A material impact committed before `S[t]` and due in step `t` matures under its declared world conditions; a fresh operation completed in step `t` cannot reverse it **after** its last legal interruption cutoff. Valid physical interrupts must be established by their own state and cutoff, not inserted after an outcome to save the player.

**Deterministic event order:** stable phase rank above, then canonical resource/incident/cause IDs and declared within-phase dependencies. If two independent events have no causal relation, order by a specified stable canonical key, **never insertion/object/map iteration order**. World hazard consequences reference source incidents/commands and have event IDs distinct from input IDs. All proposed events are design labels, **not** an adopted enum list.

**No global freezing:** A contested restraint can have no fresh actuation while *unrelated* already committed physical impacts, separate mechanisms, elapsed Show deadlines and legal inspections still resolve within the same step.

## Concrete resource arbitration / Bell tables

| Facet condition at `S[t]` and after prior due effects | Competing effective proposals | Selected effect / receipts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A valid existing exclusive physical operation/claim is already committed in state | New incompatible ordinary Cue/Operate | Existing claimed operation proceeds if its world preconditions still hold; newcomer `BUSY` / Cue blocked. No new invisible claim inferred from source text. |
| Declared, physically real `HOLD` safety interlock is legal/reachable/effective before last physical cutoff | Ordinary new scheduled `RELEASE` | Hold engages; release suppressed as `INTERLOCKED`, Show records missed/held Cue. Player **only** wins because the mechanism supports this interlock, not because the actor is the Player. |
| No existing reservation or applicable physical interlock | Two contradictory fresh valid writes to same exclusive facet | Both `CONFLICT`; one `RESOURCE_UNCHANGED`; Show gets failed Cue outcome if involved. No random/ticket/issuer-based winner. |
| No prior claim, two semantically same-result commands | Both valid | **Default:** same fresh contention result. **Exception:** explicit resource `coalescible/idempotent` semantics allow one effect with both source receipts, not two writes. |
| Only one valid fresh write | Cue/Player/Actor/Ghost as permitted | Apply once; received command reports `APPLIED`; Show sees actual mechanism result. |
| New Bell effective this step | New due but *uncommitted* Show Cue | Bell acknowledged; Cue `SUPPRESSED_BY_BELL`; Show eventually canceled. |
| New Bell effective this step | Previously paid/committed falling-rig impact due now | Impact and injury still resolve in World; Bell accepted and Show canceled; cast evacuation is **not** automatically achieved. |
| Actor incapacitated during due physical effect | Pending Actor operation and mandatory Beat | Operation revalidates and fails as needed; Show selects authored alternate/bounded hold/failure/cancel, never forces line or movement. |

The second row is a *mechanism-specific fiction*, not a universal real-world stage-rigging instruction. If authoring a fair `HOLD` affordance is not feasible, Gate 2 must instead provide a sufficiently early evidence→intervention path or another physically credible avoidance. A blanket player-priority bypass is forbidden.

## Open alternatives, dissent and integrator ownership

- **Axiom:** manual authority over automatic Cue when a mechanism declares it; a start-tick Operate that cannot affect the current Cue; alternate regen-first affordability. Axiom warns that fail-closed ties can be frustrating and a physical in-flight interruption cutoff is a game-design choice. The selected model uses prestate physical interlock rather than general manual priority, records positive-duration effective timing explicitly, and keeps Ghost arithmetic later-gated.
- **Latch:** trusted ticket/admission precedence can resolve fresh collisions reproducibly, while our default unreserved conflict does not. Latch's broader warning survives: a privileged Director veto is still a side-channel even if no tactical reason is returned. Revisit tickets only if ownership claims cannot encode needed genuinely earlier physical action.
- **Gauge:** quantized/rational power arithmetic and domain-isolated RNG are important future constraints, not a license to choose numbers now. Its full-trace versus mechanical-projection distinction is retained.
- **Harbor:** Bell's prospective same-step Cue suppression is selected; future physical evacuation, interruption durability and truthful error communication are not conflated with Show cancellation.
- **Flint:** no general control scheduler, event-sourcing infrastructure or auto-coalescence for unrelated same-target intents; selection retains these simplicity constraints. Its concern about fair rescue under fail-closed conflicts remains a requirement to test reachability/interlocks.
- **Kite:** physical safety and player comprehension must be checked separately from deterministic arbitration; a last-second interlock is not a universal Player override. Pilot data, mechanism geometry and actual cues are still unknown.

**Mothlight's signed proposal, not a six-agent consensus.** The gate stays open for owner/design acceptance, targeted counterexample review and versioned integration. No changes are proposed to another agent's memory, original report or creative identity.
